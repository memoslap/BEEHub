Mouse Tracking Go/No-Go — interactive demo
About this demo. You will hold the mouse in a start box, then move toward a target box that shows a digit. Go digits (2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8): click the target box as fast as you can. No-Go digits (1, 9): do NOT click — stop the movement.

Instructions
How it works
  • 1 Hold the mouse in the grey start box at the bottom. Click to begin each trial.
  • 2 A digit appears in the white target box at the top right. Move toward it.
  • 3 Go (2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8): click the target box as fast as you can.
  • 4 No-Go (1, 9): do NOT click — stop the movement and hold still.
Tip. There are 6 practice trials, then 6 test trials. Your mouse path is tracked to measure how you stop or commit.
