================================================================================
CONCLUSION
================================================================================

Both APPL and OLM have identical BIDS derivative structure:
- 3 outcome files per sub-ses (ACC, ACCBIN, RT)
- All files complete (no missing outcomes)
- Same core columns (onset, duration, trial_type, learning_stage, stimulus)
- APPL has extra metadata columns (signal_detection_theory, response_port)
- APPL's ACCBIN has coherence; OLM's ACCBIN does not
- OLM has 21 participants listed in participants.tsv but only 20 with data
- Both use learning_stage LS1–LS4 and trial_type learning/control
