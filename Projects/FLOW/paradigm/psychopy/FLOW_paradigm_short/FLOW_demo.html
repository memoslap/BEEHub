FLOW

Flow & mental arithmetic — interactive demo

What this is
A shortened, browsable version of the FLOW paradigm. You solve addition problems whose difficulty adapts to your performance. Blocks alternate between three conditions: boredom (too easy), flow (matched), and overload (too hard).

How it works

1 An addition problem appears. Work it out mentally.
2 Type your answer with the on-screen numpad or your keyboard.
3 Press Enter to submit. Difficulty adapts as you go.
4 After each block you rate how the task felt on a 7-point scale.
Three conditions
Boredom (B): Always the simplest task — one small number added to a value in the 100s, e.g. 104 + 7. Difficulty never changes.

Flow (F): Matched to your current skill level and adapts within the block — two correct in a row makes it harder, two wrong makes it easier.

Overload (O): Three levels above your skill level, so it stays deliberately too hard. It can ease slightly after repeated errors but never drops to your comfortable level.
